02 / A bit about me

Learning by making.

I'm studying computer science at Brown University, with an interest in both software engineering and machine learning. I like projects where I can work on the whole system: the model or backend, the interface, and the details that connect them.

My projects have taken me from reinforcement learning in PyTorch to building applications around my own music library. I primarily work in Python and Java, and use JavaScript and React for web projects.

Outside of programming, I enjoy hiking, gaming, and video editing in Premiere Pro. Usually with a boba tea nearby.
