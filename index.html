02 / A bit about me

Learning by making.

I'm studying computer science at Brown University, with an interest in both software engineering and machine learning. I like projects where I can work on the whole system: the model or backend, the interface, and the details that connect them.

My projects have taken me from reinforcement learning in PyTorch to building applications around my own music library. I work across Python, Java, C/C++, Kotlin, and TypeScript, and enjoy connecting backend systems to useful interfaces.

Outside of programming, I enjoy hiking, gaming, and making short documentaries and videos. Usually with a boba tea nearby.
